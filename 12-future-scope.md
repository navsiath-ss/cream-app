# Document 12: Future Scope & Strategic Roadmap

## 1. Strategic Roadmap Highlights

1. **Automated SynthID Web Scraping & IP Tracking**:
   - Deploy automated web crawlers to scan digital channels for leaked creator content bearing SynthID watermarks, automatically issuing DMCA takedowns.

2. **Dynamic AI Creator Ranking Engine**:
   - Enhance creator ranking algorithms by incorporating vector-embedded project metadata, on-time delivery rates, and client satisfaction metrics.

3. **Global Identity Verification Integration**:
   - Expand beyond DigiLocker by integrating global KYC partners (Onfido, Persona) for seamless international creator onboarding.

4. **Custom LLM Fine-Tuning on Negotiation Transcripts**:
   - Fine-tune proprietary LLMs on anonymized negotiation transcripts to generate increasingly precise, automated virtual contracts.

5. **Multi-User Creative Agency Teams**:
   - Introduce agency workspace features, enabling creative directors to manage multiple AI creators and client brand portfolios under centralized permissions.
