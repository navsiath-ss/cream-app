# Document 04: Platform Feature Specification

## 1. Feature Breakdown

### F-01: AI-Assisted Brief Builder
- **Description**: Natural language conversational interface that guides brand managers through brief creation.
- **Capabilities**: Automatically extracts target content type, aspect ratio, camera angles, style benchmarks, model preferences (e.g., Midjourney v6, Runway Gen-2), and commercial usage rights.

### F-02: Creator Profiles & AI Portfolios
- **Description**: Structured creator showcases tailored for generative AI production.
- **Capabilities**: Highlights AI tools/models used, specific workflows (e.g., ComfyUI pipelines, LoRA training), specialization tags, and verified sample renders.

### F-03: Creator Verification Signals
- **Description**: Trust indicators displayed on creator profiles.
- **Capabilities**: Badges for verified DigiLocker KYC, verified past commercial work, tool competency badges, and platform history.

### F-04: Context-Preserved Search & Matching
- **Description**: Programmatic search engine backed by semantic vector embeddings.
- **Capabilities**: Matches structured client requirements against creator metadata and vector-embedded portfolio attributes.

### F-05: Live AI Witness & Contract Generator
- **Description**: AI assistant that monitors negotiations between brand and creator.
- **Capabilities**: Automatically parses chat transcripts to generate binding virtual contracts covering deliverables, revision counts, timelines, and licensing.

### F-06: Protected Escrow Vault & Sample Preview Player
- **Description**: Secure media player and financial vault.
- **Capabilities**: Holds client funds in escrow; displays dual-watermarked sample outputs for client feedback and milestone sign-off.

### F-07: Hybrid Dispute Resolution Console
- **Description**: Workspace for resolving project disagreements.
- **Capabilities**: Combines AI transcript analysis with human specialist intervention for full/partial escrow refund decisions.
