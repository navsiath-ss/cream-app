# Document 13: Design Choices & UX Philosophy

## 1. Key Design Choices

1. **Natural Language Discovery over Complex Filter Forms**:
   - Replaces traditional multi-select forms with a conversational AI interface that extracts structured specs naturally.

2. **Dual-Persona AI Assistant System**:
   - Deploys separate AI client-side (brief extractor) and creator-side (brief interpreter) interfaces connected by a shared context store.

3. **Non-Bypassable Virtual Contract Gates**:
   - Embeds mandatory virtual contract sign-off UI components at every critical transition point (brief agreement, work commencement, final release).

4. **Dual Watermarked Sample Preview Player**:
   - Custom media player displaying clear visible branding and embedding steganographic SynthID metadata during client sample review.

5. **Weakly Defensible Rejections Framework**:
   - Enforces pre-approval of watermarked sample renders, establishing a clear evidentiary trail for dispute resolution.
